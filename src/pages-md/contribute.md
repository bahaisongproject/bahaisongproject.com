There are many ways in which you can contribute.

## Add or improve a song

The main way to contribute is through the [Bahá'í Songs Portal](https://portal.bahaisongs.com/submissions). You can submit a new song, add missing details, or suggest updates for songs that are already in the collection.

Create an account or sign in on the portal, then choose whether you want to add a new song or improve an existing one.
