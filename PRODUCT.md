# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Bahá'í community members, musicians, families, children’s class teachers, devotional gathering hosts, and friends looking for singable material. They usually arrive with a practical task: find a song, learn it quickly, download a song sheet, or browse a collection for an upcoming gathering.

## Product Purpose

bahá'í song project helps people discover, learn, and share Bahá'í-inspired songs through lyrics, chords, videos, song sheets, and curated collections. Success means visitors can confidently find a song and move from browsing to singing without technical friction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, generous, and communal. The site should feel like a trusted music resource: welcoming and human, but organized enough for repeated practical use.

## Anti-references

Avoid a dashboard-like SaaS surface, overly customized shadcn styling, decorative visual noise, and heavy framework complexity. The migration should simplify the technology while keeping the existing teal identity and gentle tone.

## Design Principles

- Make finding a song the first-class interaction.
- Keep the site readable and quiet enough for repeated use.
- Let collections and song metadata feel curated, not mechanical.
- Use shadcn as accessible structure, not as a new visual personality.
- Preserve stable URLs and community trust during technical migration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible controls. Search, navigation, forms, embeds, and pagination should work on mobile and desktop, respect reduced-motion preferences, and avoid relying on color alone.
